About Project

Computer Lab Inverter Management System is designed to manage inverter and battery backup information in a computer laboratory.


Objectives

  • Monitor battery backup information.
  • Maintain power failure records.
  • Store maintenance details.
  • Display inverter status.
  • Reduce manual record keeping.
